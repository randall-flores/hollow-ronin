## 🌍 THE WORLD

In the age after the last shōgun, the great houses burned.

Their warriors scattered into the smoke and were never named again. The world called them *hollow* — men without master, without house, without the right to carry a blade. They wandered the broken roads between empires, between centuries, between the living and what the living had forgotten.

Some of them died. Most of them died.

But thirteen did not.

<a name="the-protagonist"></a>
## ⚪ THE PROTAGONIST — THE HOLLOW RONIN

**Japanese name:** *Mon no Mukō* (門の向こう — "Beyond the Gate")
**Slug:** `hollow-ronin`
**Clan:** None — he walks between them
**Sigil:** `mon-hollow-ronin` (torii gate in brushstroke circle)
**Design:** The torii gate hero piece

### Lore

He walked through the torii alone.

The gate between the living and the forgotten. The threshold no one returns from. He crossed it with no master to mourn and no name worth speaking, and what came back was not a man — but a vow that refused to die.

He has no clan.
He has no face beneath the hood.
He walks among the thirteen to remember what the world made them forget.

He is the **Hollow Ronin.**

You do not find him.
He finds you.

### Tagline (product page)

*"The torii marks the threshold. The ronin chose to walk through it alone."*

<a name="the-clans"></a>
## 🏛️ THE THREE CLANS

Twelve walk the broken roads with the Hollow Ronin. Twelve who were once warriors, monks, spirits, and shadows — bound now to clans that no longer exist except in the marks they carry.

---

### 🔥 AKATSUKI-GUMI — The Crimson Clan

**Sigil:** `mon-akatsuki` (red dragon coiled around blood-red rising sun)
**Tone:** Dark / tragic / honor-bound (Berserk, Ghost of Tsushima)
**Manifesto:** *"Our masters fell. Our blood did not."*

The blood-bound. Warriors whose vows outlived their houses, who bargained with dragons and storm-spirits for one more breath, one more strike, one more name to carry into the dark.

Their mark is the dragon coiled around the rising sun.
Their color is the red that does not wash out.

**Members:** 4 — Ryūjin, Hone no Chikai, Karada-Nashi, Arashi-Maru

---

### 🌑 YAMI-GUMI — The Shadow Clan (The Hannya Court)

**Sigil:** `mon-yami` (crimson hannya inside black moon)
**Tone:** Mythological / spiritual (Princess Mononoke, folklore)
**Manifesto:** *"Behind every mask, a face we no longer recognize."*

The mask-wearers. Four masks for four sorrows the world refused to name: wrath, mourning, reckoning, stillness. To wear one is to remember what the others have forgotten.

Their mark is the hannya inside the black moon.
Their color is the shadow between sorrow and rage.

**Members:** 4 — Akuma no Ikari, Namida no Oni, Saigo no Sabaki, Mu no Kamen

---

### ⚡ KAGE-GUMI — The Phantom Clan

**Sigil:** `mon-kage` (kitsune fox skull with flame-tail crow wings)
**Tone:** Cyberpunk / spiritual-future (Ghost in the Shell, Edgerunners)
**Manifesto:** *"We were never alive. We will never be gone."*

The spirits, the foxes, the tengu, the ghosts wearing armor. Bound to vows that crossed the boundary between life and what comes after. They are not haunting — they are *remembering*. And that is worse.

Their mark is the kitsune and the crow wing entwined.
Their color is the silver of a moon that does not set.

**Members:** 4 — Kurokitsune, Yūrei, Karasu-Tengu, Shinigami

<a name="voice"></a>
## 🗣️ BRAND VOICE + STYLE GUIDE

### Voice rules

1. **Short, dense, mythic.** No filler. Every sentence carries weight.
2. **Present tense for the world. Past tense for the fall.** "He walks" / "The houses burned."
3. **Use ellipsis sparingly.** Em-dashes are preferred for breath: "He walked through the torii — alone."
4. **Japanese names first, English subtitles second.** Always.
5. **Never explain the mystery.** The Hollow Ronin's face stays hidden. His name stays unspoken. His clan stays unknown.
6. **Numbers in word form when sacred** ("the thirteen," "the four masks"). Digits when product/UX ("$38," "4D 2H").

### Words to use

bound, forgotten, vow, mask, threshold, hollow, mark, blade, ash, shadow, gate, breath, sorrow, silence, ledger, name, oath, kami, ronin, court

### Words to AVOID

epic, legendary, premium, exclusive, limited (use "closing" / "ends soon" instead), drop (only in title casing "Drop 001"), hype, fire (the slang sense), based

### Phrasing patterns that work

- "He / she / they {verb} the {noun}." — short subject-verb-object
- "{Noun}. {Contrasting noun}. {Final noun}." — triadic rhythm
- "What {action} is no longer {identity} — only {essence}."
- Manifesto stamps in italics inside quotes — *"Our masters fell. Our blood did not."*

## 🎨 COLOR + VISUAL PALETTE

### Brand colors (locked)

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Obsidian** | `#0A0A0A` | shirt base (black), site background |
| **Bone** | `#F4EDE2` | shirt base (cream/white), light text accents |
| **Blood** | `#A1182A` | brand accent, sigil red, CTA highlights |
| **Ash** | `#6B6B6B` | future grey shirt colorway, secondary UI |
| **Smoke** | `#2A2A2A` | future charcoal shirt colorway, card backgrounds |

### Rules

- **No blue. Ever.** Including blue-greys, navy, cyan, teal.
- **Red is always the accent, never the base.** Used sparingly = power. Used everywhere = noise.
- **Cream and black are the primary canvas.** Sigils + designs do the work.
- **Future colorways must be neutral** so sigils stay readable on all.
